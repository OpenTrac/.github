# OpenTrac

OpenTrac is a community dedicated to the preservation and digital archaeology
of games created by a team of developers originally working at
[SingleTrac Entertainment Technologies, Inc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SingleTrac),
and their eventual successor, [Incognito Entertainment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Incognito_Entertainment).
Their most well known works are the [Twisted Metal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twisted_Metal) and
[Jet Moto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jet_Moto) games.

The community's understanding is that the source code has been completely lost for
these games due to various complications with Sony IP deals and company buyouts across both
companies' lifespans. As a result, most of their games are unplayable today outside of
their original console or an emulated equivalent.

The community has made a dedicated effort to try and preserve the games for the future
through the process of **Reverse Engineering, Archaeology, and Reconstruction of Code (Re-Arc)**.
This process uses matching decompilation techniques to reconstruct **human-readable** source code
in the same style as it was originally written.

This organization's projects exist for research, archaeological, and preservational
purposes, and do not seek to facilitate piracy or profit from copyrighted IP or material.
